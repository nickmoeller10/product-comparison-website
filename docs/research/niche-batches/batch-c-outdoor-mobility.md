2. No usable program: DeWalt/Milwaukee/Makita, Maytronics/Pentair, Generac, most hot-tub majors, EGO US (unconfirmed), Tonal/Hydrow.
3. Highest $/sale with instant access: massage chairs, saunas/plunges, robot pool cleaners, e-bikes; all carry M-H clawback and 30-60d holds.
4. Best public-data fit: tires (UTQG), pool pumps (ENERGY STAR/WEF), e-bikes/scooters (UL 2849/2272), coolers (IGBC), golf (USGA lists), drones (FAA/FCC). Tools, mowers, massage chairs, fitness have weak public data.
5. Only "best electric bike" (~29.9k) surfaced in a snippet; other volumes are estimates. Lead-gen is the natural second stream for hot tubs, pool, EV chargers, golf sims, drones.
