Batch notes:
1. Impact hosts most brand programs (EcoFlow, Jackery, Anker, Whynter, Wolfbox, Pelican/Pentair, Home Depot, Lowe's); Awin/FlexOffers carry Coway, Sylvane, Kind, Hunter; Rakuten programs migrating to Impact (April 2026 alliance); ShareASale closed Oct 2025. In-house programs (Emporia, Sense, HVACDirect, Big Ass Fans, Signature Solar, SpringWell) approve fastest.
2. Lead-gen: Modernize $9.60/HVAC lead starter tier; EnergySage $9.60/solar lead; other solar PPL to $75; HVAC publisher payouts $20-60/lead plausible, unverified. Lead-gen carries high scrubbing risk and TCPA obligations.
3. Amazon: Home/Home Improvement/Lawn & Garden 3%, Automotive 4.5%, Tools 5.5%. Home Depot 1% and Lowe's 2% with 24h cookies are near-useless for big-ticket HVAC.
4. Best data moats: mini-splits (AHRI, NEEP, ENERGY STAR), EV chargers (ENERGY STAR EVSE), dehumidifiers/purifiers (AHAM CADR), water heaters (AHRI UEF). Best money: whole-house water (10-20%, $150-300/sale), DIY solar (5-9% on $2-4k), power stations (5% on $1k with heavy promo cadence).
5. Suggested pairing: a "home energy backbone" of power stations, DIY solar/batteries, EV chargers, energy monitors (same buyer, same networks, UL/ENERGY STAR data), with mini-splits and heat-pump water heaters as lead-gen extensions.
