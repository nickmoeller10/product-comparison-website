## Public data
- Fiberglass: California AB 1059 bans fiberglass in mattresses and upholstered furniture from Jan 1 2027 (only
  enacted state law); New York bills pending; CPSC opened a fiberglass hazard investigation with public FOIA
  releases listing mattress fiberglass reports.
- CPSC SaferProducts: documented fiberglass complaints (Casper, Ashley, Zinus class action settled 2023);
  2025-26 CPSC flammability warnings and recalls on Amazon-sold mattresses (Elitespace, EVLWZL).
- Flammability: 16 CFR 1632 and 1633 mandatory; CPSC recalls list violators.
- Certification lookups: CertiPUR-US directory (and its list of non-compliant claims); OEKO-TEX Label Check;
  GOTS database; GOLS certified list; GREENGUARD Gold via UL SPOT.
- Foam density: brands that publish: Saatva, Amerisleep, partially Brooklyn Bedding/Titan; most do not.

## FTC and litigation
- Resident Home (Nectar, DreamCloud, Awara, Level Sleep): 2018 order and Oct 2021 $753k consent order for
  false USA-made claims.
- Consumer Review Rule: Dec 2025 warning letters (unnamed); no mattress brand or review site publicly named.
- Private "fake sale price" class actions: Purple (2024, ongoing), Nectar (2024). Relevant to how a comparison
  site presents reference prices.
