Notable findings:
- Mattresses: the famous high payouts are DreamCloud and Nectar only; Saatva, Helix, and Tempur-Pedic pay far
  less at base; four of the top review sites share one owner; returns exceed 20 percent and commissions are
  held through 100-night trials. Wrong niche for a data site.
- Power stations: specs fully public and comparable (Wh, W, chemistry, cycle life, UL 2743); returns low;
  incumbents are generalist tech sites. Owned-data angle: measured usable capacity and recharge curves.
- HVAC: the best public data of any niche (ENERGY STAR, AHRI); refrigerant transition and expired 25C
  credit create decision content; retailer rates thin but Sylvane and HVACDirect pay 5 to 6 percent.
- Appliances: LG's rate is more likely 5 to 6 percent than 12; Wayfair 7 percent with a 7-day cookie is the
  surprise; Yale Appliance's service data is the strongest first-party competitor.
- E-bikes: Rad Power went bankrupt Dec 2025 after refusing a CPSC recall; CPSC proposed a mandatory battery
  standard June 2026; a database tracking UL 2849 certification has a regulatory story.
- Finance: highest per-approval payouts and the hardest hit in the March 2026 update (NerdWallet -15.9%,
  Credit Karma -34.2%, Motley Fool -32.1%).
- Amazon 2026: cuts up to 50 percent in some categories, bonuses removed, commission restricted to the exact
  linked ASIN variant.
- FTC: 10 warning letters in Dec 2025 under the reviews rule; penalties up to $53,088 per violation.
